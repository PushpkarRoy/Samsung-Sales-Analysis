## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Samsung’s sales, supply chain, procurement, and operational performance using **SQL, Excel, and Power BI**.  

The goal is to transform raw data into **actionable business insights** that support decision-making for stakeholders like **CFO, Operations Manager, and Sales Head**.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze **sales performance and revenue trends**
- Evaluate **profitability and cost efficiency**
- Identify **supply chain inefficiencies**
- Monitor **inventory and procurement performance**
- Provide **data-driven recommendations**

---

## 🧠 Key Business Questions

### 📊 Sales & Profitability
- Which products generate **highest revenue but low profit?**
- What is the **gross margin by product?**
- Is the company **growing sustainably?**

### 📦 Supply Chain & Operations
- What is the **shipping reliability %?**
- What is the **average lead time?**
- Is **production aligned with sales demand?**

### 💰 Cost & Procurement
- What is the **ROI of procurement spending?**
- Are we improving profitability despite **rising costs?**
- What is the **procurement cost trend?**

### 📦 Inventory & Risk
- Do we have **excess inventory or stock shortage?**
- What is the **inventory holding cost impact?**
- What is the **biggest operational risk?**

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- 📌 Some products generate **high revenue but low profit**, indicating **pricing or cost issues**
- 📌 Increasing **unit cost trends** impact overall profitability
- 📌 Delayed shipments reduce **customer satisfaction and operational efficiency**
- 📌 Excess inventory leads to **high holding costs and blocked capital**
- 📌 Supplier delays may increase **production cost and inefficiency**

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Optimize **pricing strategy** for low-margin high-revenue products  
- Reduce **procurement and production costs** through supplier negotiation  
- Improve **shipping performance** to increase customer satisfaction  
- Maintain **optimal inventory levels** to reduce holding costs  
- Improve **demand planning and forecasting accuracy**
